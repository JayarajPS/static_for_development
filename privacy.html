Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information from you in the following ways:

  • Voluntary Information: Name, email address, or contact details that you provide when filling out forms or contacting us directly.
  • Automated Information: Log files, IP addresses, browser types, and visiting patterns collected via basic analytics tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Operate, improve, and maintain our website.
  • Respond to your direct inquiries, comments, or customer service requests.
  • Analyze website traffic and usage trends.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses basic cookies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings; however, doing so may affect certain features of this website.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with trusted service providers who help us run our website, or when required by law to comply with legal obligations.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement basic security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access or disclosure. However, please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
